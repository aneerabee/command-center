## Design Context

### Users
Rabee uses this personal Arabic workspace to open and follow his projects, companies, services, tools and team across desktop and mobile. Existing project records and operational links must stay available.

### Brand Personality
Clear, composed, practical. The owner requested a complete redesign that improves organization and daily usefulness, not just decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
The owner chose a calm light interface. Light neutral workspace and navigation, restrained teal actions and separate amber/red status colors. Arabic-first typography with IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Compact headings, unframed sections, structured lists, and progressive disclosure.

### Design Principles
- Opening a project and finding its current evidence should be immediate.
- Derive counts from the current inventory; old evidence must never look live.
- Preserve every entity and existing deep link.
- Keep all sections accessible on mobile and by keyboard.
- Use color for state and navigation, not decorative gradients.
